# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone contributing to Enterprise Dealer Service Hub.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different opinions and experiences.
- Provide constructive feedback.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Respect other contributors.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or disruptive behaviour.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project maintainer through GitHub.

All reports will be reviewed fairly and respectfully.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related interactions.
